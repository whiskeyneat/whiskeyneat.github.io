Whiskey Neat, LLC

Paid Media Management & Conversion Rate Optimization

Whiskey Neat, LLC plans, builds and manages advertising campaigns for a small number of clients, and does the conversion work that decides whether those campaigns were worth running. Services include paid media management on Google Ads, Meta and LinkedIn, landing page and funnel optimization, ad creative development and structured creative testing, and campaign measurement and reporting.

The company has operated since 2016 and takes on new clients primarily by referral.

Enquiries: chuck@whiskeyneat.com

Whiskey Neat Ads Reporting

Whiskey Neat Ads Reporting is internal software used by this company to retrieve advertising performance data from the Google Ads accounts it is authorized to manage.

The application authenticates through Google OAuth as the account owner and reads reporting data — cost, impressions, clicks, conversions, keyword performance and search terms — so that it can be assembled into daily and monthly performance summaries.

It is read-only. It does not create, modify, activate, pause or delete campaigns, ad groups, ads, keywords, budgets or bids, and it cannot authorize advertising spend. It is used only by Whiskey Neat, LLC, on accounts this company is engaged to manage, and it collects no information from anyone other than the authenticating user.
